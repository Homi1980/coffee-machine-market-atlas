Model-level origins are estimates from Nespresso FAQ, Keurig packaging and supplier disclosures, and Dolce Gusto product pages; the same model can be made in more than one country, and origin can vary by unit even within a range. Unit splits by model are modeled, not measured.

Machine types. Green coffee = machines that roast raw green beans (home roasters and roast-grind-brew units, e.g. Aillio, Behmor, Gene Café, Kaffelogic, Fresh Roast) — a niche well under 1% of units. Roast & ground / beans = drip/filter, manual espresso, bean-to-cup fully automatics, Senseo-style open pads are counted as pods. Pods & capsules = Nespresso, Keurig K-Cup, Dolce Gusto, Tassimo, Senseo, Lavazza A Modo Mio etc.
Method and sources. Country volumes and seller shares are modeled estimates, anchored on published figures: De'Longhi ~18% and top-5 players ~47% of global value (GM Insights); Keurig 28% / Nespresso 18% of North American automatic machines (GM Insights NA); Europe ~38.6% of value and filter machines ~31% (Mordor Intelligence); Keurig >18M machines/yr, De'Longhi ~3.5M fully automatics/yr, Nespresso >22M machines operating globally (SNS Insider); Dolce Gusto shipped 4.7M machines globally in 2023, with 63% sold in emerging markets (Glass and Note). China's seller mix follows published reports: De'Longhi ~24%, Nespresso ~8%, Philips ~7%, Gemilai ~6% of value (GM Insights China), Nespresso ~2.6% of units (Moojing), with Joyoung, HiBREW, Barsetto, Petrus and Xiaomi as major local sellers (ICE/ITA). Country-of-manufacture splits reflect publicly known plant footprints (e.g. De'Longhi in Italy, Romania and China; Jura/Nespresso machines from Eugster/Frismag, which assembles mainly in Portugal, Hungary and China with limited Swiss output; Keurig brewers are assembled in Malaysia, Thailand, Indonesia and China; most drip and pod brewers from Chinese OEMs) and are approximate. Nespresso and Dolce Gusto machines are sold under partner brands (De'Longhi, Krups, Breville) — they are counted under the system owner here. Use for directional analysis, not as audited data.
